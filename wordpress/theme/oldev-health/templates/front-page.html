Six categories

What gets checked

Each category gets a score out of 100. The report ranks every problem it finds, most urgent first.

  • TLS certificate

    Certificate validity and expiry

  • Security headers

    Security headers such as HSTS and Content-Security-Policy

  • WordPress

    WordPress fingerprint and version exposure

  • Search basics

    Page title, meta description, canonical link, robots.txt and sitemap

  • Email authentication

    SPF, DKIM and DMARC records for your domain

  • Performance

    PageSpeed Insights performance on mobile

Scans are passive. They read what any visitor can already see. They do not scan ports, test passwords or attempt to exploit anything. Reports are deleted after 30 days.
